About Me

A bit about who I am and where I come from

Hi! My name is Guillem and I was born in Manresa in 2002, a city about an hour driving from Barcelona. I speak fluent Catalan, Spanish and English. I'm currently located in Rome as part of the second year of the EMAI program, so I'm trying to learn a bit of Italian as well. My main hobbies include playing the viola and practicing sports. I also enjoy cooking (particularly pizzas) and hiking.

My Trajectory

As a kid, I found pleasure playing chess and building "legos". With some practice, I won the youth regional tournaments and later (with some more practice) earned a place on the youth Catalan chess team. Around that age I always assumed I'd become an engineer, since it was the closest thing to the idea of "invention" but at some point my focus shifted to music, specifically to the viola.

Around my teens, I spent most of the afternoons at the conservatory of music of Manresa and in several youth orchestras (including the JOSV and JONC). After 6 years of work, I graduated from Professional studies in Viola. At that point I was torn between pursuing music and pursuing maths and physics. Just a couple of months before finishing high school, I decided to go for the second option. I'm not entirely sure what drove that decision, but I don't regret it. From there on, I found the same excitement in learning mathematics that I once found in other arts such as music. I graduated in the top 5% of my class, with a publication in photonics and honors in my B.Sc. thesis in mathematics, and made some great friends along the way.

Afterwards, I decided to pursue a master's degree. After some thought, I chose to enrol in the EMAI master's jointly offered by UPF, Radboud University, the University of Ljubljana, and Sapienza University of Rome because of contents and the prestige. There I met a cohort of 36 talented and passionate students from 27 different countries which am I currently sharing my daily life with. In the future I want to pursue a Phd in an area on the intersection of maths and AI.
